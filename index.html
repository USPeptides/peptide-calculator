Instructions:
Mix 5 mg of peptide with 2 mL of bacteriostatic water. For each 250 mcg dose, draw 0.100 mL (pull syringe to 10 units).

ℹ️ How It Works

1. Concentration

Divide vial amount (mcg) by water volume (mL) to get concentration (mcg/mL)

2. Volume Needed

Divide desired dose by concentration to get injection volume

3. Syringe Units

Convert mL to syringe units for precise measurement

Example

  • 5 mg vial + 2 mL water = 2.5 mg/mL
  • Want 250 mcg (0.25 mg)?
  • 0.25 mg ÷ 2.5 mg/mL = 0.1 mL
  • 0.1 mL = 10 units on 1mL syringe
